Our story

More than a renovation.

SIHINA is the Annual Radiography Day Celebration Project carried out by the Society of Radiography and Radiotherapy at General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University. The project continues a long-standing community-service tradition.

The 2026/2027 initiative focuses on improving the learning environment at Urugala Sri Dharmaloka School through renovation of an old classroom complex.

At a glance

Organizer: Society of Radiography and Radiotherapy, Intake 40

Institution: Faculty of Allied Health Sciences, KDU

Location: Urugala Sri Dharmaloka School, Ingiriya, Horana

World Radiography Day: 8 November

What we're building

A better place to learn.

The proposal identifies renovation of the old classroom complex as the main requirement. Planned work includes:

🎨PaintingFloor paint, weather shield, filler, skim coat and lacquer work.
💡ElectricalWiring, switches and plug bases.
🌀Ceiling fansThree ceiling fans.
🟩Green boardsThree 8×4 green boards.
🧱Floor & buildingRepair and renovation works.
🪑ClassroomFurniture and colour-washing improvements.
📚O/L seminarSeminar planned for GCE O/L students.
🤝CommunityStudent-led service for the school community.
